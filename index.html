About Me

I am a Research Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at HKUST. My research centers on online learning and optimization. I study sequential decision-making problems, including bandits and auctions, as well as optimization on Riemannian manifolds.

I received my Ph.D. in Machine Learning from Georgia Tech, advised by Jacob Abernethy. Previously, I obtained my master's degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University and my bachelor's degree from Huazhong University of Science and Technology.

Research Interests

  • Operations research and online decision making
  • Machine learning theory and bandit algorithms
  • Riemannian optimization
